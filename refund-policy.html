01. Our Approach to Refunds

GetKit offers digital resources such as templates, bundles, tools, guides, and other downloadable products. Because digital products may be delivered or accessed shortly after purchase, refund requests need to be assessed carefully.

We do not offer an unconditional refund guarantee for every purchase. Instead, we review requests individually, consider the facts, and aim to resolve genuine problems fairly.

This policy applies to purchases made through GetKit, subject to any specific terms displayed for a product and the requirements of applicable law.

02. When a Refund May Be Considered

You can contact us if you believe something went wrong with your order. Depending on the circumstances, a refund may be considered in situations such as:

01

Duplicate payment

You were charged more than once for the same order and the additional charge is confirmed.

02

Missing product

You paid successfully but did not receive the product you purchased, and delivery cannot be resolved.

03

Defective files

A file is corrupted, materially incomplete, or does not work as described, and a reasonable fix is unavailable.

04

Incorrect transaction

There is a verified billing or order error that warrants a refund or correction.

These examples are not an exhaustive list. A refund or another appropriate remedy may also be required under applicable law. Where possible, we may first offer a working download, replacement file, or another suitable solution if you agree and it resolves the issue.

03. When Refunds Are Not Automatic

The following circumstances do not automatically qualify for a refund, although each request will be considered in context and subject to applicable law:

  • You changed your mind after receiving or downloading a digital product.
  • You purchased the wrong product or misunderstood details clearly provided in the product listing.
  • You expected a feature, file, update, or service that was not included in the product description.
  • You cannot use a product because your device, software, or system does not meet its stated requirements.
  • You no longer need the product or purchased it by mistake.
  • You have already downloaded or used the product and are requesting a refund without another valid reason.

Please review product descriptions, included files, and compatibility details before purchasing. We will not use this section to deny a remedy that you are legally entitled to receive.

04. Download & Access Problems

If you have purchased a product but cannot find your download, please do not assume that your money is lost. Delivery emails can sometimes end up in spam or promotions folders.

1

Check your email

Search your inbox, spam, junk, and promotions folders for your Lemon Squeezy receipt or download email.

2

Check your order confirmation

Look for download instructions or an order link provided after checkout.

3

Contact GetKit support

Email us with your order reference and the email used at checkout. We will help investigate the issue.

If the problem cannot be resolved, we will review the circumstances and consider any appropriate remedy, including a refund where warranted or legally required.

05. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund review, email our support team. Providing the details below helps us investigate your order more efficiently.

Include these details in your email

  • Your order number or transaction reference, if available.
  • The email address used during checkout.
  • The product name you purchased.
  • A clear explanation of the problem and the outcome you are requesting.
  • Relevant screenshots or error messages, if helpful. Hide any sensitive payment details.
Email a Refund Request ↗

Please contact us using the email associated with your order where possible. We may ask for reasonable information to verify the transaction before discussing account or order details.

Never send your password, full payment-card number, card security code, or banking credentials.

06. Review & Processing

We will review the information you provide and may ask follow-up questions where necessary. We aim to respond within 3–5 business days, although complex cases or periods of high demand may take longer. This is a response target, not a guarantee of a decision within that period.

If a refund is approved, it will generally be processed through the original payment method or the applicable checkout provider's refund process, where supported. The time it takes for the funds to appear may depend on the payment provider, bank, card issuer, and payment method.

A request does not guarantee approval. If we cannot approve a request, we aim to explain the reason where appropriate. Nothing in this section limits remedies or timelines required by law.

07. Payment Provider & Transactions

GetKit uses Lemon Squeezy for checkout and payment processing. Some order, payment, and refund actions may need to be handled through its systems.

Contact GetKit at support@getkit.in first so we can help identify the correct next step. If the checkout provider requires you to follow its own process, we will direct you to the relevant channel where possible.

Refund decisions and processing remain subject to applicable law and any relevant checkout-provider requirements. We cannot guarantee a bank's or payment provider's processing speed.

08. Your Legal Rights

This policy describes GetKit's general approach to refund requests. It does not override mandatory consumer protection laws or other legal rights that apply to your purchase.

If applicable law gives you a right to a refund, cancellation, replacement, repair, or another remedy, nothing in this policy is intended to take that right away.

We may update this policy when our products, checkout arrangements, or legal obligations change. The version published on this page will include the date of the latest revision.

09. Need Help? Contact Us

If you have a question about this policy or need assistance with a purchase, please reach out. We would rather help investigate a genuine issue than leave you unsure about your order.
