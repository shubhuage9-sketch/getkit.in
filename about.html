Our story

What is GetKit?

A growing library of things you can actually use.

GetKit brings different kinds of digital resources together in one place, including templates, bundles, tools, software resources, games, and other practical things you can actually use.

Our goal is simple: make useful digital resources easier to find and affordable enough for more people to try.

Some resources are made specifically for creators, developers and businesses. Others are simply useful for everyday users.

The beginning

Why I started GetKit

It started with a pretty simple idea.

There are countless templates, tools, resources and digital products online. But finding the right ones isn’t always easy. And for someone who is just starting out, spending a lot of money on resources isn’t always an option.

I wanted to create something different — a place where you could find useful digital resources, explore different kinds of products, try some things for free, and buy what you actually need without having to spend a huge amount.

That’s how GetKit started.

The story behind GetKit

I’m building GetKit one step at a time.

Hi, I’m Shubham, the founder of GetKit. I’m a creator and developer from India, and GetKit started from something I personally wanted to see.

I’ve seen how many useful templates, tools, resources and digital products are available online, but finding the right ones — and being able to afford them — isn’t always easy.

So I started building GetKit as a place where useful things could be collected and offered at prices that don’t make people think twice about trying them.

It’s currently a small, independently built project, and I’m the person behind it. I’m building it, improving it, adding resources and learning along the way.

GetKit is still growing. We’re not trying to pretend that everything is perfect today. We’re building, learning, improving and adding better resources over time.

✦

We’re not perfect — and we’re okay saying that.

GetKit is still growing. We aren’t going to tell you that everything on the website is already the best resource you’ll ever find. We’re building this step by step.

Some resources will be better than others. Some things will improve over time. And as GetKit grows, we want to add more useful, better-made and higher-value resources.

If you support GetKit today, you’re supporting something that’s still being built. And that’s something I genuinely appreciate.

Our mission

Make useful digital resources more accessible to more people.

GetKit started in India, but the idea isn’t limited to one country. There are creators, developers, freelancers, students, entrepreneurs and businesses all over the world who need useful resources but may not always have a huge budget.

Small beginning. Bigger goal.

What we’re building toward

GetKit is just getting started.

Over time, I want to build a much larger collection of useful digital resources, better bundles, more free resources, better tools and higher-value products.

But I don’t want to build a website that is simply filled with thousands of random files.

I want GetKit to become a place where you can come looking for something specific and actually find something worth using.

That’s the direction we’re working toward.
