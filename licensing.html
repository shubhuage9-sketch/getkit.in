1. Overview

This Licensing & Usage Policy explains the general rules for using digital products obtained through GetKit.in ("GetKit", "we", "us" or "our"). These products may include templates, design resources, code, guides, downloadable files, bundles and other digital resources.

By purchasing or downloading a product, you agree to follow this policy, the product's individual licence terms and any applicable third-party terms. If a product page or included licence provides specific terms that differ from this general policy, the product-specific terms govern that product to the extent of the difference.

This policy does not grant rights that GetKit does not own or have permission to grant.

2. What your licence means

Purchasing or downloading a digital product generally gives you access to the product under the licence that applies to it. It does not automatically transfer ownership of the underlying copyright, source material, brand, or intellectual property to you.

Unless the product listing or its included licence says otherwise:

  • Your permission to use a product is limited to the rights expressly granted by its applicable licence.
  • A purchase does not automatically include resale, redistribution, sublicensing or commercial redistribution rights.
  • Some products may be limited to personal use, while others may permit specified commercial uses.
  • Any restrictions or additional permissions stated on the individual product page or in the included licence must be followed.

3. Personal use

Where the applicable licence permits personal use, you may use the product for your own learning, personal projects, organisation, productivity or other permitted non-commercial activities.

For example, where allowed by the product's licence, you may be able to:

  • Use a template to organise your personal work or studies.
  • Customise a permitted resource for your own projects.
  • Use a guide to learn a skill or improve your workflow.
  • Keep a permitted downloaded copy for your own use, subject to the licence and any applicable access conditions.

Personal-use permission does not allow you to publish, distribute or sell the original product as a standalone resource unless the applicable licence expressly permits it.

4. Commercial use

Some GetKit products may be suitable for business, client or commercial projects. However, commercial use is not automatically included with every product.

Before using a resource in paid work, client projects, business operations, marketing or a product you intend to sell, check the product description and its licence. If commercial permission is not clearly stated, contact us before relying on the product for commercial purposes.

Where the licence allows commercial use, that permission applies only within the licence's stated limits. It does not necessarily permit you to sell, share or distribute the underlying files themselves.

5. Restricted activities

Unless the applicable licence expressly allows an activity, you must not:

  • Resell, re-upload, redistribute, share or publicly publish the original product files.
  • Offer purchased files as your own downloadable product, template, bundle or resource library.
  • Repackage a product and sell it, give it away or distribute it as a competing resource.
  • Share paid download links, account credentials or restricted access with unauthorised people.
  • Remove copyright notices, attribution requirements or other ownership information where removal is not permitted.
  • Claim that another creator's work is your original creation or that you own rights you have not acquired.
  • Use products in a way that violates applicable law, third-party rights or the product's own terms.

These restrictions do not prevent you from using a product to create your own permitted end result. For example, using a template to make a presentation is different from reselling the original editable template file, subject to the applicable licence.

6. Ownership and copyright

GetKit and its respective creators, licensors or third-party providers retain ownership of the intellectual property they own in the products, including applicable copyright, trademarks, designs and other protected material.

Your licence does not transfer that ownership to you. You may own original content you create yourself, subject to any rights belonging to other people whose materials are incorporated into your work.

GetKit may distribute products created by third parties. In those cases, the original creator or rights holder may set the applicable licence conditions. We do not claim ownership of third-party intellectual property merely because a product is listed on GetKit.

7. Free products and resources

Free does not automatically mean unrestricted. Free downloads may still have copyright protections, attribution requirements, usage limits or restrictions on redistribution and commercial use.

Check the relevant product page, included documentation and any licence file before using or sharing a free resource. If no licence terms are provided and the intended use is unclear, contact us for clarification.

8. Third-party products and services

Some resources may include or depend on third-party software, fonts, images, plugins, APIs, services or other materials. Those components may be subject to separate licences, terms of service, usage limits or payment requirements.

You are responsible for reviewing and following the relevant third-party terms before using those components. A GetKit purchase does not automatically include a separate third-party subscription, paid account, commercial licence or ongoing service unless the product listing expressly says so.

9. Purchases, downloads and ongoing access

A product purchase generally provides the specific product and access described on its product page. GetKit does not promise lifetime access, permanent hosting, future updates, new releases or ongoing support unless the applicable product listing expressly includes those benefits.

Keep your downloaded files and purchase confirmation in a safe place where the product licence allows you to retain them. Download links, hosting arrangements, external services and access methods may be subject to technical or provider limitations.

For help locating a purchase or resolving a download issue, visit our Purchase & Download Support page or email support@getkit.in.

10. Licence violations

If we receive credible information that a product is being used in violation of its licence or the rights of its creator, we may investigate and take reasonable action where appropriate and legally permitted.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include contacting the relevant person, requesting that unauthorised distribution stop, restricting access where permitted, or referring a rights complaint to the appropriate provider. Any action will remain subject to applicable law and the rights of the parties involved.

Nothing in this policy removes any rights or remedies available under applicable law.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our products, services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Changes to this general policy do not automatically expand the licence granted for a previously purchased product. The product-specific licence and applicable law continue to matter.

12. Questions about a licence

Not sure whether you can use a product for a client project, business, commercial design or another purpose? Contact us before using it in a way that may fall outside the stated licence.
