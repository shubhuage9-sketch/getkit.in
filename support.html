Troubleshooting

Let's fix it step by step.

✉️

I purchased a product but didn't receive an email

Your order confirmation and download details may be sent to the email address you entered during Lemon Squeezy checkout. Try the following:

  1. 01 Check your Inbox, Spam, Junk, and Promotions folders.
  2. 02 Search for “Lemon Squeezy”, “GetKit”, or the product name.
  3. 03 Check the email address you used when placing the order.
  4. 04 Still can't find it? Contact us with your order details so we can help you with the next step.

My download link or file isn't working

Try opening the download link in an updated browser, check your internet connection, and attempt the download again. If the file downloads but won't open:

  • • Check the file extension and the application needed to open it.
  • • If it is a ZIP file, extract it before opening its contents.
  • • Review the product description for software requirements.
  • • If the download is incomplete, try downloading it again.

If the problem continues, tell us the product name and exact error message. A screenshot of the error can also help; hide any personal information first.

My payment went through, but I can't find my order

Check whether your payment is completed or still pending, then look for a Lemon Squeezy confirmation email. If your payment appears completed and the order details are missing, contact us with the product name, purchase date, checkout email, and any order reference available.

For your safety, never email your full card number, CVV, banking password, or OTP. Check your existing order before attempting another purchase.

Common Questions

Purchase & download FAQ

Where can I find my download? +

Check the order confirmation or download email sent after checkout. Follow the download instructions provided for your particular product.

I entered the wrong email at checkout. What should I do? +

Contact support and explain the issue. Include the product name, purchase date, and any order reference you have so we can guide you through the next step.

Do I get lifetime access to every purchase? +

GetKit purchases do not automatically include lifetime access. Check the individual product description for what is included and the access terms that apply.

How do I ask about a refund? +

Email support@getkit.in with your order details and the reason for your request. Refund eligibility is subject to the applicable GetKit policies and the circumstances of the purchase.
