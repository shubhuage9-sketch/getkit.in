01. Overview

Welcome to GetKit.in ("GetKit", "we", "our", or "us"). GetKit is a digital resource platform offering products and resources such as templates, bundles, tools, deals, games, and practical guides.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, or shared when you visit our website, purchase a product, or contact us. We aim to handle personal information responsibly and do not intend to sell it for money.

By using our website, you acknowledge this policy. Where consent is legally required, we will seek it as appropriate.

02. Information We Handle

The information involved depends on how you use GetKit and which services are enabled on the website. It may include:

  • Information you provide: your name, email address, and the contents of messages you send to our support team.
  • Order information: purchase confirmations, product details, order references, and transaction status supplied by our checkout provider.
  • Technical information: limited device, browser, IP address, and access-log information that may be processed by website hosting or security services.
  • Website usage: interaction or performance information if analytics, cookies, or similar tools are enabled.

We do not ask you to provide sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely necessary for a specific, clearly explained purpose. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other confidential information in ordinary support messages.

03. Payments & Orders

GetKit uses Lemon Squeezy for checkout and payment processing. When you purchase a product, the checkout provider processes the information required to complete the transaction and may send us order-related information needed to deliver your purchase and provide support.

Your payment information is handled according to the checkout provider's processes and policies. Do not assume that GetKit itself processes or stores full payment-card details.

To learn how Lemon Squeezy handles information, review its Privacy Policy. The provider may process information under its own terms and legal obligations.

04. How Information Is Used

Where applicable, information may be used to:

  • Complete purchases and help provide access to purchased digital products.
  • Respond to questions, requests, complaints, and refund or order-related enquiries.
  • Verify order details and investigate delivery or download problems.
  • Maintain website functionality, security, and reliability.
  • Understand website performance if analytics tools are enabled.
  • Meet applicable legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory requirements.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorised activity, or misuse of our services.

We aim to use information only for relevant purposes and to avoid collecting information we do not need.

05. Sharing Information

We do not intend to sell your personal information. Information may be shared when reasonably necessary with:

  • Service providers: hosting, checkout, payment, website security, or other services needed to operate the site.
  • Legal authorities: where disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal process.
  • Protection and security: where necessary to investigate fraud, protect rights, or address misuse.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy terms. We cannot guarantee the privacy practices of services that are independently operated by other organisations.

06. Cookies & Website Services

Cookies and similar technologies may be used by our website, hosting provider, checkout provider, or other integrated services to support essential functions, security, preferences, or analytics.

The technologies actually used depend on the website configuration. We do not claim that analytics, advertising cookies, or tracking tools are absent unless that has been verified.

You may be able to manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect some website features or checkout functions. Where required by law, consent will be requested before using non-essential tracking technologies.

07. Security & Data Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through our website and business processes. However, no website, online service, or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information may be retained for as long as needed to provide services, resolve support issues, maintain appropriate business records, prevent fraud, or meet legal obligations. Retention periods may differ between GetKit and third-party providers.

08. Your Choices & Privacy Requests

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with you, or raise concerns about how it is handled.

To make a privacy-related request, email support@getkit.in. Please explain your request and provide enough information for us to understand it. Do not include passwords or full payment-card details.

We may need to verify your identity or locate the relevant order before responding. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate business purposes.

09. Children's Privacy

GetKit is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate steps.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, business practices, or legal obligations change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated date.

We encourage you to review this page periodically so you understand how information may be handled.
